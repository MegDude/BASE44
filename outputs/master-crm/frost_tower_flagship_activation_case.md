# Frost Tower Flagship Activation Case

Segment: Property

Position Frost Tower as a downtown workplace and district activation anchor, not a generic office pin.

Core activation modules:
- DAA Art Walk and Downtown Stories route proximity
- Nearby lunch, coffee, happy hour, civic, and event discovery
- Office-worker check-ins, saves, directions, and campaign handoffs
- Sponsor/brand packages tied to Congress Avenue foot traffic
- Partner workspace reporting for route opens, stop engagement, and nearby conversions
