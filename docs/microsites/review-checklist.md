# Microsite review checklist

A reviewer must confirm every item before a record can become public.

- Correct organization or place identity
- Public-safe summary and headline
- Verified location where relevant
- Approved relationship language
- No private contact or relationship notes
- No unsupported offer, event, result, or ROI claim
- Approved media rights
- Unique canonical route
- Appropriate CTA
- SEO title, description, and canonical URL
- Mobile QA at 393 × 852
- Accessibility and link validation
- Explicit release approval
