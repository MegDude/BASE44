The opportunity

A city can have everything and still feel disconnected.

Downtown Austin already has remarkable restaurants, independent retailers, hotels, residential communities, parks, cultural institutions, and civic organizations.

What it has lacked is a shared way to connect them around the moments that make a neighborhood useful: where to go, what is happening, who is nearby, and what is worth returning to.

The neighborhood does not need more noise. It needs a clearer way in.

The experience

Downtown Perks connects what already makes downtown worth choosing.

The beginning

One focused beginning.

Every partnership begins with one audience, one useful experience, one approval path, and one measurable objective.

Define the opportunity

Agree on the audience, desired behavior, approval path, and evidence that would make expansion worthwhile.

Build the experience

Create the partner page, map presence, campaign or offer, workspace, QR destination, and launch materials.

Launch through trusted channels

Use resident email, portals, hotel communications, lobby signage, venue QR, partner channels, or an event already earning attention.

Learn from real behavior

Review profile views, map opens, saves, directions, bookings, scans, redemptions, participation, repeat use, and operating effort.

Grow what works

Refine, repeat, expand, or pause based on evidence rather than enthusiasm alone.

Contact and verification rules
  • Never infer or fabricate a personal email.
  • Use a warm path, official inbox, property line, or contact form when a direct route is unavailable.
  • Separate the person who can approve from the person who will operate and report.
  • Mark every route as verified, internal, proposed, or awaiting confirmation.
  • Unknown participation is not program membership; unknown authority is not approval.
Pre-merge QA checklist
  1. Run the restaurant/inKind taxonomy regression test.
  2. Run the map panel action-rail and QR visibility regression test.
  3. Open Dining and confirm all restaurant pins use the knife-and-fork glyph.
  4. Open inKind and confirm only the explicit cohort appears.
  5. Confirm a non-inKind restaurant perk does not create inKind membership.
  6. Test Show QR with one, two, and three preceding actions on mobile and desktop.
  7. Run typecheck and production build before merge.
