How it works

Four steps from sign-up to live neighborhood data.

Walk through each one below. This interaction is included in the deployable clone.

Step 1 of 4

Connect your property

Five minutes. No IT department. No excuses. We set up your property profile, connect it to the neighborhood map, and turn on the perks layer for your people.

Your building appears on the live map immediately. The people around you start seeing your property as part of the neighborhood — because it already is.
Step 2 of 4

People find their way in

Residents, guests, and neighbors discover nearby places, events, and offers without downloading another bloated app.

Step 3 of 4

The neighborhood shows up

Properties, venues, and local activity appear in the same layer, so context does the persuasive work instead of a pile of disconnected pages.

Step 4 of 4

The numbers tell the story

Scans, saves, RSVPs, and redemptions reveal how the layer is influencing behavior while it is still useful to know.

Principle 1

Show what actually happened

Useful data belongs on screen while you are drinking your coffee, not buried in a PDF later.

Principle 2

Everything connects

Coffee shop, building, pop-up, district. Downtown does not work in silos, and neither should your tools.

Principle 3

Earn every open

No decorative dashboards. No fake engagement loops. Useful or nothing.
