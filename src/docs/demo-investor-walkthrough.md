# Downtown Perks Demo + Investor Walkthrough

## Recommendation

Run the demo as one clean movement: open the map, tap one useful option, show the decision drawer, then switch to partner value. Do not tour the product. Do not explain every route. The point is to prove that Downtown Perks turns nearby intent into action.

## Demo Promise

Downtown Perks is a real-time decision layer for downtown Austin. Residents open the map, see what is useful nearby, and act in one tap. Buildings and partners see what residents actually engage with.

## Assumptions

- Audience: building owners, residential operators, local partners, sponsors, or early investors.
- Demo device: laptop first, phone second.
- Demo length: 5 to 7 minutes.
- Primary route: /downtown-perks/explore.
- Backup route: /.

## Five-Minute Walkthrough

### 1. Open with the problem

Say: Downtown has plenty of places, perks, events, and building activity, but residents still decide in fragments. They search, text friends, scroll social feeds, or miss what is nearby.

### 2. Show the resident moment

Open the map. Do not scroll. Point to the map and say: This is the product. It is not a directory. It is the operating layer for what is useful nearby right now.

### 3. Tap one pin

Tap one relevant map pin. Let the drawer open. Say: The resident does not need to compare twenty things. The system narrows the decision and gives one useful next action.

### 4. Explain the action

Show the drawer actions. Say: From here they can save it, go there, redeem a perk, or RSVP depending on the type of place.

### 5. Connect to partner value

Say: Every useful action becomes a privacy-safe signal. Buildings see what residents respond to. Venues see what drives attention. Brands see where demand is forming.

### 6. Close with the business model

Say: The pilot is simple. We launch a resident-facing map layer for a building or district, activate local partners, and measure engagement over a defined pilot window.

## What Not To Show

Do not open every route. Do not show settings. Do not explain backend architecture. Do not describe ranking logic in technical terms. Do not scroll through long lists. Do not use the word dashboard until the resident value is obvious.

## Investor Framing

The wedge is resident utility. The business is local engagement infrastructure. The advantage is that Downtown Perks captures intent at the exact place and time a resident is deciding what to do.

## Partner Framing

For buildings, this becomes a resident amenity and engagement signal layer. For venues, it becomes nearby demand capture. For sponsors, it becomes measurable local activation.

## Success Signals

- A first-time viewer understands the product in under 10 seconds.
- The main resident action takes 3 clicks or fewer.
- The demo stays on the map for most of the walkthrough.
- The viewer asks about pilots, buildings, partners, or pricing instead of asking what the product is.

## Demo Script

Where downtown works like a system.

Downtown Perks helps residents find what is useful nearby right now. The map is the product. A resident opens it, taps what is relevant, and acts in one step.

For buildings, that same activity becomes a signal layer. They can see what residents engage with, what nearby partners matter, and where activation is working.

The first pilot is not about proving every possible feature. It is about proving one core loop: open, decide, act, measure.
